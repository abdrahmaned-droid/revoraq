How to read this. Profit margin is profit as a share of your price. Markup is profit as a share of your cost — the same sale always shows a bigger markup than margin, so never quote one and price off the other. Break-even units are rounded up, because you can't sell a partial unit. REVORAQ calculations run entirely in your browser and are not sent or saved.
